title: Set Up Billing and Add Credits
description: Sign in, manage a personal or team plan, and choose who pays for cloud model use.

---

Cloud models require an LM Studio account with available credits. Local and LM Link models do not use Bionic cloud credits.

## Sign in

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **General**, find **Account**.
3. Select **Sign in** and finish signing in through your browser.
4. Return to Bionic.

## Add credits for personal use

1. In **Settings** under **Account**, select **Manage plan**.
2. Add credits or manage your plan on the LM Studio account site that opens.
3. Return to Bionic and open **Billing and Usage**.
4. Select **Refresh** if the new balance is not shown yet.

Bionic does not currently have an in-app credit purchase form. Purchase steps and labels on the account site may change.

Under **Billing account**, choose **Personal** to charge cloud model use to your personal balance.

## Manage a plan

Bionic can show plan labels such as **Free**, **Pro**, **Teams**, and **Enterprise**. Select **Manage plan** to view or change the available plan on the LM Studio account site.

## Use an organization billing account

If your account is eligible to use an organization, it appears under **Billing account** as `@organization`.

1. Open **Settings** → **Billing and Usage**.
2. Open **Billing account**.
3. Select the organization.

Future cloud requests use the selected billing account. Only organizations available to your signed-in account are listed.

The Bionic organization view shows your usage, but it does not show the organization's remaining credit balance.

See [Understand Credits and Usage](/docs/bionic/accounts-plans-and-billing/credits-and-usage) for the usage fields shown in Bionic.
